Human Resources Analytics Dashboard
📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Human Resources (HR) analytics dashboard designed to provide a comprehensive view of workforce data and support data-driven HR decision-making. The dashboard focuses on employee demographics, hiring trends, turnover, and workforce distribution across departments and locations.
The report enables HR teams and management to quickly identify patterns, monitor key HR metrics, and gain actionable insights related to hiring, retention, and workforce composition.

🎯 Objectives
•	Analyze hiring trends over time
•	Understand employee distribution by gender, age group, race, department, and location
•	Monitor employee turnover across departments
•	Support strategic HR planning and workforce optimization


________________________________________
📊 Dashboard Features & Insights
1. Hiring Trends
•	Percentage Hire Change (2000–2020)
•	Displays long-term hiring growth trends
•	Highlights periods of increase and decline in hiring activity
2. Workforce Diversity
•	Gender Distribution (Male, Female, Non-Conforming)
•	Race Distribution across major demographic categories
•	Age Group Distribution (21–30, 31–40, 41–50, 50+)
•	Age Group Distribution by Gender for deeper demographic insights
3. Department Analysis
•	Gender Count by Department
•	Identifies gender balance across departments such as Engineering, Accounting, HR, Sales, and more
•	Turnover Rate by Department to highlight retention challenges
4. Location & Work Model
•	Remote vs. Headquarters Employees
•	Employee Distribution by State using geographic visualization
5. Job Role Insights
•	Top Job Titles by Employee Count
•	Helps identify the most common and critical roles in the organization
